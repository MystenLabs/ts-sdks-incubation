---
title: private-content
description: Walrus blob storage + Seal threshold-encryption + capability-gated decrypt.
---

End-to-end private content flow. Plaintext is encrypted with Seal client-side, blobs are uploaded to
Walrus, and a capability object on Sui mediates who can request decryption keys. Exercises the full
sui+walrus+seal stack — the heaviest example in the repo.

[`examples/private-content/`](https://github.com/mysten-incubation/devstack/tree/main/examples/private-content)
