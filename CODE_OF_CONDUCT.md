# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind, be honest, assume good faith, give credit, and own your mistakes. Engage with
ideas, not the people behind them. If you wouldn't want it said to or about you in a
public forum, don't say it about someone else.

## Scope

Applies to anyone participating in this project — issue threads, pull request reviews,
discussions, chat, and offline interactions tied to this work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination based on any protected characteristic.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory language directed at participants.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of others' positions or work.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or off-topic posting after being asked to stop.
- Sharing private communications without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this policy and may
ban contributors for repeated or egregious violations. Decisions are at maintainer
discretion and may be appealed by emailing `security@mystenlabs.com`.

## Reporting

Report concerns to `security@mystenlabs.com`. All reports are handled confidentially.
We commit to acknowledge receipt within two business days.
